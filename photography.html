Infrared · Barbican, London

Barbican Dreamland

A study in Infrared Photography.

Shot at Barbican Conservatory using a specially adapted Sony A7 set up with an infrared adapter on the sensor and one on the lens, to pick up light that we humans can't see. The result, following some digital processing, is a beautiful dreamscape of pinks instead of the expected greens.
